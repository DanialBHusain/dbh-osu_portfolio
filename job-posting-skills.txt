The only real thing that surprised me was how many years of relevant experience you need for both positions. Another thing that surprised me was how much was different between the two positions even though they're technically in the same position. The final thing that I’d say surprised me was the similar requirements of having experience with optimisation models/algorithms, as I never really considered that to be an aspect of Data Science.
